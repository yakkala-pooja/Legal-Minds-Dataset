Chapter Name : PROCESSES TO COMPEL APPEARANCE
Section Name : Summons
Title : Service of summons on corporate bodies and societies
Description : 
Service of a summons on a corporation may be effected by serving it on the secretary, local manager or other principal officer of the corporation, or by letter sent by registered post, addressed to the chief officer of the corporation in India, in which case the service shall be deemed to have been effected when the letter would arrive in ordinary course 
of post.
Explanation.—In this section, “corporation” means an incorporated company or other body corporate and 
includes a society registered under the Societies Registration Act, 1860 (21 of 1860).
