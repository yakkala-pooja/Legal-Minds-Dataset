Chapter Name : PROCESSES TO COMPEL APPEARANCE
Section Name : Proclamation and attachment
Title :  Appeal from order rejecting application for restoration of attached property
Description : 
Any person referred to in sub-section (3) of section 85, who is aggrieved by any refusal to deliver property or the 
proceeds of the sale thereof may appeal to the Court to which appeals ordinarily lie from the sentences of 
the first-mentioned Court.
