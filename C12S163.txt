Chapter Name : INFORMATION TO THE POLICE AND THEIR POWERS TO INVESTIGATE
Title : No inducement to be offered
Description :
(1) No police officer or other person in authority shall offer or make, or 
cause to be offered or made, any such inducement, threat or promise as is mentioned in section 24 of the Indian Evidence Act, 1872 (1 of 1872).
(2) But no police officer or other person shall prevent, by any caution or otherwise, any person from making in the course of any investigation under this Chapter any statement which he may be disposed to make of his own free 
will:
Provided that nothing in this sub-section shall affect the provisions of sub-section (4) of section 164.
