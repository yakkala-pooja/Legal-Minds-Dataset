Chapter Name : PROCESSES TO COMPEL APPEARANCE
Section Name : Summons
Title : Service when persons summoned cannot be found
Description : 
Where the person summoned cannot, by the 
exercise of due diligence, be found, the summons may be served by leaving one of the duplicates for him with some 
adult male member of his family residing with him, and the person with whom the summons is so left shall, if so 
required by the serving officer, sign a receipt therefor on the back of the other duplicate.
Explanation.—A servant is not a member of the family within the meaning of this section.
