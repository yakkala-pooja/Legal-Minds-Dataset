Chapter Name:PROCESSES TO COMPEL THE PRODUCTION OF THINGS 
Section Name:General provisions relating to searches
Title: Direction, etc., of search-warrants.
Description:
The provisions of sections 38, 70, 72, 74, 77, 78 and 79 
shall, so far as may be, apply to all search-warrants issued under section 93, section 94, section 95 or 
section 97.
